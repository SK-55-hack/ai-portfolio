About Me

Technology, imagination, and the stories in between.

I'm drawn to the point where technology meets imagination. Through AI-assisted visual creation, cinematic storytelling and short-form video, I try to turn an abstract feeling into something a viewer can see, hear and remember.

I'm currently studying B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (AI), with a CGPA of 8.79/10. That technical foundation helps me understand how generative tools work, where they break, and how to shape them into something intentional rather than generic.

"I'd rather make a small film that feels true than a big one that feels empty."

I'm building this portfolio through independent creative projects. Each one is a self-directed exercise in concept, direction and editing. I'm looking for opportunities where I can keep developing this work with a team that values emotional storytelling and careful craft.
